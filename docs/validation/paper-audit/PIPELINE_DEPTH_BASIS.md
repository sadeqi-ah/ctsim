## 2. Table V Pipeline Depth: Candidate Bases

The paper defines effective pipeline depth by Little's law:
$\Cdepth = \text{Throughput} \times \text{Latency}$.
The manuscript text (`:1902-1905`) and caption (`:1907-1909`) claimed that
Table V was obtained by applying Little's law directly to the columns of
Table I (`tab:baseline`).

Using raw data from `plots/topology/results/sweep_summary.csv` at the reference
operating point (`random`, $\Nnodes = 27$, $\text{loss} = 0.05$, 15 seeds), with
$\text{throughput} = \text{committed} / \text{end\_slot}$ per
`plots/topology/plot_topology.py:94`:

### Candidate Basis Formulations
1. **Product of Means:**
   $\text{mean}(\text{throughput}_i) \times \text{mean}(\text{avg\_latency}_i)$
2. **Mean of Per-Seed Products:**
   $\text{mean}(\text{throughput}_i \times \text{avg\_latency}_i)$
3. **Median of Per-Seed Products:**
   $\text{median}(\text{throughput}_i \times \text{avg\_latency}_i)$

### Finding
The **mean of per-seed products** (`mean(throughput_i * avg_latency_i)`) is the
ONLY candidate basis that reproduces all six published values:
- For the four non-pipelined combinations (TOM CE, Paxos CE, 2PC CE, TOM CI),
  $\text{end\_slot} = \text{committed} \times \text{avg\_latency}$ holds on
  every seed identically, so throughput $\times$ latency $= 1.00000000$ exactly
  on every run.
- For Paxos (CI), the mean of per-seed products is `11.66152391` $\to$ `11.66`.
  (The product of aggregate means gives `11.68504943` $\to$ `11.69`).
- For 2PC (CI), the mean of per-seed products is `21.25465016` $\to$ `21.25`.
  (The product of aggregate means gives `21.31454078` $\to$ `21.31`; the median
  gives `21.43387097` $\to$ `21.43`).

### Repository Evidence for Origin of `11.66` and `21.25`
Searching for `11.66` and `21.25` across committed files confirms they were
generated by code using the mean of per-seed products:

  `depth: runs.csv / sweep_summary.csv, (committed/end_slot) x avg_latency,`

- `docs/validation/addition7/scripts/make_patch_spec.py:81, 120, 177`:

## 3. Table I Sleep Column Audit

### Column Availability in Sweep Summary CSV
Field 17 of `plots/topology/results/sweep_summary.csv` (and identically in
`plots/scalability/results/sweep_summary.csv`) is named `sleep`.

### Reference-Point Computation
Aggregating the `sleep` field as the arithmetic mean across the 15 seeds at the
reference operating point (`random`, $\Nnodes = 27$, $\text{loss} = 0.05$):
- TOM (CI):   mean `3899.8667` slots $\to$ rounded integer **`3900`**
- Paxos (CI): mean `4434.2667` slots $\to$ rounded integer **`4434`**
- 2PC (CI):   mean `5033.7333` slots $\to$ rounded integer **`5034`**
- TOM (CE):   mean `0.0000` slots $\to$ **`0`**
- Paxos (CE): mean `0.0000` slots $\to$ **`0`**
- 2PC (CE):   mean `0.0000` slots $\to$ **`0`**

### Pipeline Generator Origin
In `plots/scalability/plot_summary_commit.py:85`, the summary table is built
with `sleep=("sleep", "mean")` and rounded to 0 decimal places (`:103`).
The resulting table is committed at
`plots/scalability/table_summary_N27_loss05.csv:2-7`:
```text
TOM (CI),15,...,3900.0
Paxos (CI),15,...,4434.0
2PC (CI),15,...,5034.0
TOM (CE),15,...,0.0
Paxos (CE),15,...,0.0
2PC (CE),15,...,0.0
```

### Verdict
**REPRODUCIBLE from committed data.** The Table I `Sleep` column values are the
exact integer roundings of the 15-seed means of the `sleep` column from
`sweep_summary.csv`.

## 4. Manuscript Claim Correction Requirement

For the manuscript's claim about Table V's derivation to be mathematically true:
- The text must NOT claim depth is obtained by applying Little's law directly
  to the displayed aggregate columns of Table I (which would yield 11.69 and
  21.31).
- The text and caption must state that depth is computed via Little's law per
  seed and averaged over the fifteen seeds (the mean of the per-seed products),
  matching the actual computation.
