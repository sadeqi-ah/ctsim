## Answers to the eight charges of STOP #6

The charge numbering follows the review. Three of the eight (9, 12, 15) were
conclusions contradicting the table above them; all three are conceded and rewritten
in the items above. Charge 16 is the reconciliation gate, answered first because
decision 40 makes it a precondition.

### Charge 9 — conceded. The conclusion survives, the argument does not.

"None of the three is separable from zero" is false, and the next sentence in that
report said so. Paxos-CI's interval is [+0.000125, +0.031009] and 2PC-CI's is
[+0.001342, +0.059066]; both exclude zero. Only TOM-CI's includes it.

The t-interval was also the wrong instrument: `f` is a share, non-negative by
construction, and a t-interval on a skewed non-negative quantity manufactures a
negative lower bound that cannot exist. The sign test is reported instead — 15 of 15
positive, p = 3.05 × 10⁻⁵ — and the question restated as magnitude, where the answer
is no: relative half-widths 99.2 % and 95.6 %.

**The part that cuts against the conclusion, stated plainly.** The amortised estimator
is the *more precise* of the two, by **2.55×** for Paxos (0.0263 against 0.0671) and
**2.10×** for 2PC (0.1306 against 0.2737) on the ratio's half-width. That is expected:
amortising divides out exactly the tail-length variance that the cumulative metric
carries. So the cumulative metric cannot be defended on precision, and the defence
rests on three non-statistical grounds:

1. **The mechanism is dead.** The tail is denser than the run average, not asleep
   (item b). "Amortise it because the radio is off anyway" has no physical basis left.
2. **The correction's magnitude is not known to a printable digit** (99 % and 96 %
   relative half-widths), so a corrected headline would carry false precision.
3. **`f` is definition-sensitive** — see charge 10, where the aborting proposals move
   2PC's `f` from 3.30 % to 4.57 %.

### Charge 10 — resolved. The gap is the sixteen non-committing proposals, and `f` has two definitions.

The reconciliation fails because `f` as reported counts only slots *no window covers*,
while the amortised mean divides by *committed* decisions. A proposal that never
commits still contributes to `C_t` — it absorbs a share of every slot it is in flight
for — and then contributes no `E_i`. Its energy leaves the numerator without leaving
the denominator.

| arm | orphan node-slots | share of awake | non-committers | `f` slots-only | `f` with orphans |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOM-CI | 0.0 | 0 % | 0 | 0.000849 | 0.000849 |
| Paxos-CI | −0.0 | 0 % | 0 | 0.016624 | 0.016624 |
| 2PC-CI | **2183.2** | **1.271 %** | **16 in 5 seeds** | 0.032999 | **0.045712** |

With the second definition the identity closes on every arm:

| arm | cumulative | amortised | predicted from `f` slots-only | gap | predicted from `f` with orphans | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOM | 1.434823 | 1.436042 | 1.436042 | 0.000000 % | 1.436042 | 0.000000 % |
| Paxos | 3.827738 | 3.892446 | 3.892446 | 0.000000 % | 3.892446 | 0.000000 % |
| 2PC | 5.671896 | 5.949407 | 5.865450 | **+1.431 %** | **5.943588** | **+0.098 %** |

Both hypotheses in the charge were right about 2PC and the residual 0.098 % has a third
cause. **Per seed the identity is exact** — `mean(E_i) = (E/committed)·(1 − f)` to
1.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ on all 45 rows — but the ratio of *means across seeds* is not the mean of
ratios, and `cumulative` and `f` are positively correlated across seeds:
r = +0.29 / +0.83 / **+0.91**. The leftover 0.098 % is that covariance, not a defect.
Paxos's earlier 0.110 % was the same thing, which is why aborts alone could not
explain it.

Under decision 36 no amortised digit is printed, so this is closed as a diagnostic:
**the two paths use different denominators, aborted proposals do contribute energy
without contributing to the count, and the per-seed identity is exact once `f`
includes them.**

### Charge 14 — tested. The coincidence claim was wrong, and there is a bound.

225 runs, five values of N, fifteen seeds each, from the scalability sweep's own N
values:

| arm | N = 6 | 13 | 27 | 54 | 188 | pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOM-CI | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | **1 on every run** |
| Paxos-CI | 3 | 6–7 | 13–14 | 27–28 | 77–95 | ≈ N/2, never above |
| 2PC-CI | **6** | **13** | **27** | **54** | **100** | **= min(N, 100) on all 15 seeds** |

**`max C_t = 27` at N = 27 is not a coincidence — it is `min(N, num_proposals)`.**
2PC-CI hits it on every seed at every N below the proposal count, and at N = 188 it
saturates at 100 = `num_proposals`. The mechanism is `src/sim/two_pc_pipeline.rs:131`,
`round_num >= p.start_round + (num_nodes as u64)`: a transaction stays pending for
exactly N rounds, so N of them are in flight at once. Paxos tracks ⌈N/2⌉ instead
because `check_quorum` at `src/protocol/paxos.rs:102` releases at
`bitmap.len() / 2 + 1` votes rather than waiting for unanimity.

So the earlier assertion is withdrawn and replaced by a structural result with a
file:line. Note this is a *different* standard of evidence from TOM's `C_t ≤ 1`, which
remains measured-only: here the bound is visible in the code, there it is not.

### Charge 16 — resolved, and no number moved. Two different quantities carried the same name.

The regression gate is intact and `runs.csv` reconciles cell for cell (top of this
document). What differed was the *quantity*, not the data:

| name | patch-list value | Addition 7 value | what it measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| `f` TOM | 0.000794 | 0.000822 | **slot** share `drain/S` vs **energy** share `1 − inside/total` |
| `f` Paxos | 0.015319 | 0.015567 | same |
| half-width Paxos | 0.015726 | 0.015442 | same |
| cumulative 2PC | 5.6719 | 5.7040 | **ratio of means** vs **mean of per-seed ratios** |

Both recomputed here from `sweep_summary.csv` alone: the slot shares reproduce the
patch-list numbers to six decimals (0.00079365 / 0.01531928 / 0.02997260 against
0.000794 / 0.015319 / 0.029973), and the energy shares reproduce Addition 7's. The
ratio between them is 1.036 / 1.016 / 1.008 — the drain tail's duty ratio, item (b),
which is exactly why they differ.

The cumulative triple is the same story: 5.671896 is the **ratio of means** and
5.704037 is the **mean of per-seed ratios**, both computed from the published sweep.

**Paxos's separability verdict did not flip on the data.** It flipped because the
slot-share half-width (0.015726) is marginally wider than its mean (0.015319) while
the energy-share half-width (0.015442) is marginally narrower than its mean
(0.015567). Two quantities differing by 1.6 %, straddling a threshold. Under charge
9's sign test the verdict is the same for both: positive in 15 of 15 seeds.

Convention for everything downstream, stated once: **`f` means the energy share
including orphans, and every ratio is a ratio of means.** Where a slot share is meant
it will be called a slot share.

### Section 0 of the review — both confirmations verified
